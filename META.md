# META

## Game Concepts

There needs to be an expansive set of stats and skills for the player. This is why the game is designed around low demand for the system.

If the game runs well on low end, it allows a high degree of complexity in the game system.

The underlying logic of the game should be nearly equivalent to a terminal-based game.

Some kind of grid/cell based system will describe the space that entities exist in. There will be an overworld, local fields, cities, dungeons, specific rooms, and other areas. The overworld may be the only area that is distinctly different in terms of it's spacial representation. There will also be alternate/under worlds accessible later on.

Entities will be independent of the player. The player will be equivalent to any other entity in the game.

While this is close to a JRPG in description, that is not the intent. Something closer is the early fallout games. The reason for this is that the game is meant to be compelling on the basis of it's narrative and interaction with entities. Lots of reading and writing is a core part of the game. It is meant to be like an interactive storybook. Some kind of system will present in the game to allow the player to even write something in the game that will be remembered by the world and potentially affect it.

The setting is fantasy. However, firearms are present but rare. Any firearms are going to be stylized in a western-era equivalent.

The closest comparison to the game I have in mind is actually early diablos. A modern equivalent is the recent wasteland games.

However, running on a low-demand budget means it will open the door to a game world that is massive.

Combat will be turn based, and deep, but the PC will be able to devote their entire character to potentially non-combat skills. If they do this, they will be able to pair with companions to make up for this.

## Game Themes

The game emphasizes complexity, immersion, simulation, and narrative.

An entire, extremely massive world will be simulated. The player will be one entity that is a part of this. They can interact in the world in a variety of ways that have different scopes. They will get to choose whether they are simply another part of the world, or want to take a more active, center role. They can choose to live a simple life, or become a major hero or villian.

## Graphical System

Visually, the game will emphasize 'text first, graphics second.'

The graphical system is intentionally low demand. It will be outright pixel based potentially, evocative of early terminal/dos games. The furthest graphical stage will be equivalent to that of the early fallout or diablo games. Potentially sprites will be used.

This is intentional to allow the game to be very complex, and be accessible to develop.

This isn't to say the game will have no striking visuals, but it will sacrifice this to an extreme to allow greater complexity.

The game world will be intended to be herculean in scale. The player will be nearly insignificant.

## Audio

For now, there will be no voice acting. Building the whole game from text as a starting point is essential to the design, and is important for accessibility. The game will have music and sound effects, but they will be low demand.

## Player Character System

**Levels**
Every entity will have a level. Leveling will be limited up to a very high value. It will be possible to reincarnate to level 1, or possibly sub-levels.

**Rankings**

Rankings will be important to entities. They are a separate scaling system independent of levels. Rankings will be what distinguishes entities from not just their level but also the range of their capability. The player will start at a particular ranking, that is low, but 'special' in a way that sets them apart from other entities. They will recognize other entities in the world by their ranking.

Rankings will not necessarily indicate power, which is tied much more closely with levels. But rankings will be representative of the potential power of an entity overall.

There will be a 'trash' level of rankings. 'Fodder, peon, and minion.'

Somewhere after this there will be a 'normal' level of rankings. 'Citizen, commoner, and soldier.'

Somewhere above this there will start to be a special level of rankings. One is 'agent.' Which possesses a basic kind of specialty. There will likely be a special pre-agent level that the player begins in, then advances into the agent level.

**Character Creation**
When a new player character is created, they will go through a character creation process.

## Party System

Parties will organize by size. Up to three entities will comprise a duo or team.

Having more than three entities will require that the leader of the party have leadership and management skills.

Parties may be very small, or they may be very large.

Party sizing: 1-3 (duo or team), 4-6 (squad),

Ranking influences party size limits because ranking is a measure of the entities' independence and capability. The player will make a choice between having a small party of highly ranked entities, or a larger party of lower ranked entities.

A 'core' party will generally be 1-3 entities.
